Using this site

The information on this site is general plumbing guidance for homeowners. It is not a diagnosis of your specific problem and it does not replace an on-site visit by a licensed plumber.

Prices and scheduling

We give a clear price before work starts, and the final scope depends on what the plumber finds on site. Same-day service depends on availability.

Do it yourself work

Plumbing and gas work can cause injury and property damage. Permit rules vary by town, so ask the Millburn building department before you start a project. Call a licensed plumber for anything involving gas, sewage or the main water line.
