Victorian and Jazz Age houses, where leaks hide

Houses from these eras often have bathrooms and kitchens that were added or moved over the decades. Each change adds joints and old connections, and a slow weep at one of them can run behind plaster for months before a stain shows up on a ceiling.

A water bill that climbs with no change in habits is the usual first clue. A hissing sound with every tap closed is another. This is where leak detection earns its keep, using pressure testing and acoustic listening to locate the leak before a wall is opened.

Quiet signs of a hidden leak

  • A water meter that keeps moving with every fixture shut off
  • Warm or damp spots on a floor, which can mean a leaking hot line
  • Peeling paint or a musty smell on a wall near a bathroom
  • Sudden drops in pressure at a single fixture or on a whole floor

Read the meter before you call

Shut every tap and appliance, then watch the meter dial or low-flow indicator for a few minutes. If it still turns, water is escaping somewhere. Tell the dispatcher what you saw.

Before we arrive in West Orange

Shut off the main water valve

It is usually where the line enters the building, and in a garden apartment it may sit in a shared utility room. Turn it clockwise to stop a burst or leaking line, and ask a building manager if you cannot find it.

Cut power near wet areas

Older homes can have panels in damp basements. Switch off circuits to wet rooms only if you can reach the breaker on dry ground. If water is near the panel itself, stay back and tell the dispatcher.

Clear the area and protect valuables

Lift rugs, books and boxes off the floor under a leak, and set a bucket where water is dripping. Pull furniture away from walls that feel damp, and photograph the damage before cleanup.

Garden apartments and post-WWII modern homes

Garden apartment buildings share risers and drain stacks between units, so a leak you feel in your kitchen can start in someone else's bath. Post-WWII modern homes often add a different test, because additions and finished basements have been built over earlier plumbing.
