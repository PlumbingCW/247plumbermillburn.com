Drains and sewer lines

For a slow sink or tub, start with drain cleaning. When the same line backs up again, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall with pressurized water instead of just poking a hole through the blockage.

Sewage in a basement floor drain is a different story. A camera sewer inspection shows whether the main needs sewer line cleaning or a real sewer line repair, and that call decides how big the job will be.

Water heaters and boilers

Hot water complaints split into two groups. A heater that runs but delivers lukewarm water often has a failed element, a bad thermostat or sediment, and water heater repair is the first call. A tank leaking from its body is finished, so shut the cold supply valve above it.

Boilers behave differently, since cold radiators or a lockout can come from the burner, a circulator pump or low system pressure. A plumber checks each cause before deciding whether the fix is small or the unit has reached the end of its life.

Repair or replace, and how to decide

A repair fits when one part has failed and the rest of the system is sound. Think of a worn faucet cartridge, a toilet flapper or a bad heating element. Fixtures like these are classic faucet repair, toilet repair and garbage disposal repair jobs.

Signs replacement belongs on the table

  • A water heater tank leaks from the body, not just a fitting
  • The same fixture or pipe has failed more than once
  • Supply pipes are corroded, flaking or leaving rusty water
  • Repair parts cost close to the price of a new unit
  • Tank water is never quite hot enough for the household

When a tank is done, you can choose another tank through water heater installation or step up to a tankless water heater. Pipes that keep failing may call for repiping, and new fixtures or lines fall under plumbing installation. A plumber should look before quoting, and we do not price sight unseen.

What counts as an emergency

Gas smell comes before everything

Leave the building, move at least 350 feet away, and call PSE&G at 1-800-880-PSEG (7734) or 911. Call us after it is cleared.

Plumbing is an emergency when water or sewage is actively causing damage or when a household loses something it cannot do without. That includes a burst pipe, sewage backing up and a boiler that fails in cold weather, which is a job for boiler repair. A sump pit rising during a storm belongs here too, so see sump pump repair.

A damp patch that is slowly growing is urgent but usually not flooding, and leak detection can often be scheduled same day when available. Planned projects such as basement waterproofing can wait for a normal appointment. Businesses with a failed line can reach the same dispatch line for commercial plumbing.
